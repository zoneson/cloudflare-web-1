## What the best personal sites have that your request didn't mention

I compared it against well-known personal sites from designers, developers and consultants. These are the gaps, and the page already covers them:

1. **A one-sentence promise right at the top.** Say who you help and what you do for them, not just your job title. Visitors decide within a few seconds whether to keep reading.
2. **A clear next step.** The "Get in touch" button is at the top and again at the bottom.
3. **Proof, not just claims.** There are numbers ("10+ years"), a result for every project ("32% more orders"), and quotes from real people.
4. **Availability.** "Open to new projects starting in December" tells people whether it's worth reaching out.
5. **How fast you reply.** "I usually reply within one working day."
6. **Link previews.** The page includes the hidden information that makes your link show a title, description and picture when shared on WhatsApp or LinkedIn.
7. **A downloadable résumé** and links to your social profiles.
